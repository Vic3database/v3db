**Goal:** Build a complete Victoria 3 1.13.10 base database and versioned site data, compare it with 1.13.9, then rebuild Victorian Century content against the new vanilla baseline.

**Architecture:** Keep `database/vic3_1.13.9` and `site/versions/1.13.9` unchanged as the historical baseline. Generate `database/vic3_1.13.10` and `site/versions/1.13.10` as independent outputs from the installed 1.13.10 game files. Only after the base outputs pass checks will the VC content merge and VC site use 1.13.10 as their baseline.
